FOUNDRY YARD

Single-player tactical FPS. Five hostiles hold the foundry yard. Four modes, a full loadout, perks, operator skills and killstreaks — everything runs offline against bots.

v2.0

🎮 GAME MODE

Plant the charge at a bomb site, defend it until it blows — or clear every hostile.

🗺️ MAP — 7 maps, click to preview

Houses have interiors: open doors with [E], climb the stairs, shoot from the 2nd-floor windows.

Mythic skins glow, shed particles and change your killfeed. Gold Tier 5 · Neon 10 · Crimson 25 · Frost 35 · Rainbow 40 · Void 45 · Dragon 50. Dragon kills summon a 3D dragon, Void kills open a portal.
